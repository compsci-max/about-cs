Hardware Cleaning / Maintenance

  • Use compressed air to blow dust out of vents and fans.
  • Wipe down screens with a microfiber cloth and specialized screen cleaner.
  • Keep liquids and food away from the keyboard and computer case.
  • Ensure proper airflow around the tower to prevent overheating.
