How Screen Time Affects Kids

Technology is a big part of kids lives today. Phones, tablets, computers, TVs, and video games can be useful, but too much screen time can also cause problems.

What technology is considered for this issue?

This issue is about digital technology such as:

  • Phones
  • Tablets
  • Computers
  • Televisions
  • Video games
  • Social media

Images: Lawson Early Childhood School and Doctopedia.

Why is this an ethical issue?

It is an issue because kids need to use technology in a healthy way. Too much screen time can take away time from sleeping, learning, and spending time with family.

Parents and technology companies also have to think about how to keep kids safe and healthy online.

Who does it impact the most?

This issue mostly affects children and teenagers because they use technology every day.

It can also affect parents, teachers, and families because they have to help kids create healthy technology habits.

What are the trade-offs?

Positive effects

  • Technology can help kids learn.
  • Kids can communicate with friends and family.
  • Games can be fun and creative.
  • The internet gives kids access to information.

Negative effects

  • Too much screen time can affect sleep.
  • Kids may spend less time being active.
  • It can make it harder to focus.
  • It can replace time with family and friends.

What are the secondary harms/effects?

Too much screen time can cause other problems. For example, a child may stay up late using a phone. Then the child may not get enough sleep.

Less sleep can make it harder to focus at school. Too much sitting can also replace exercise and outdoor activities.

What are your recommendations?

I think kids should have a balance between technology and activities away from screens.

  • Take breaks from screens.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Spend time outside.
  • Exercise and stay active.
  • Spend time with family and friends.
  • Avoid using phones right before going to sleep.

Technology is not always bad. The important thing is learning how to use it in a healthy and responsible way.
